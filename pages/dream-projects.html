#1 — Modular Cyberdeck

Hardware · Raspberry Pi · Mechanical Keyboard · DIY

A fully custom, hand-built portable computer — not a laptop, not a tablet, something entirely mine. Raspberry Pi brain, sliding screen, full-depth mechanical keyboard, trackball, battery power, and swappable modules so it adapts to whatever I'm doing. The whole appeal is that it's upgradeable, repairable, and designed around how I work — not how a manufacturer thinks I should work.


This video from Jankbu is exactly what I mean. His build is the benchmark I keep coming back to:



What's blocking me right now: cost of the mechanical keyboard components, PCB fabrication, and the machining/3D printing work for the chassis. Eventually.
