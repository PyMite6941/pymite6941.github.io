How I Work


Build to learn, not to demo

Every project starts with a problem I actually have. The Finance Kit began as a school submission; LLM Protector came from needing to test local models. I push until it's production-ready — CI, tests, deployed, documented — because that's where the real learning happens.

Offline-first, security-minded

tinyGPT, MedicalAI, North Star, the Finance Kit's offline mode — I build for environments without connectivity or API keys. Security isn't an afterthought: LLM Protector red-teams models; Petri sandboxes untrusted code; the Cyberdeck is air-gapped by default.

Self-taught ≠ solo

I learn by building, but I ship with others. Polaris Student: leading a 3-person QA team across 13 modules, 8 languages. Neurole: modernizing a live product with a CSV-driven content pipeline. Hackathons: solo and team entries, all deployed. Client work: from ARGs to Stripe+Coinbase billing systems.

Show the work, not the resume

This site is the resume. Every skill listed on the Projects page is counted from real project cards — click a skill chip to see the evidence. No self-assessment bars, no "proficient in X" without a repo to prove it.
