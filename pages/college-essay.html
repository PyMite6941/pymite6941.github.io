Personal Statement

Common Application Class of 2027 Computer Science


Everyone is born into a cage—the cage of their own mind—locked in by societal pressures. I am no different; yet, over time, I have learned how to break free from that lock. Still, we are all bound by the rules set by the so-called jailer: the rules of formality, which so many of us long to escape. To fly is to be free from these expectations, to pursue our true calling and potential.

Like a raven, I have learned to study a lock and find ways to break it open—much like the work of cybersecurity, where complex problems become engaging puzzles with the right tools. Yet, every time I manage to unlock the cage, I find myself confined once again. Someone always reinforces the lock, removing its vulnerabilities, and the jailer is quick to return me to where they believe I belong. My greatest wish is to fly and experience the freedom that comes with it, but there are always those determined to keep me grounded. Over time, escaping and being recaptured has become a game—a cycle of brief freedom followed by confinement. I have even discovered ways to convince others to unlock the cage, provided I return when my time aloft is finished. Despite my frustration with these limits, I am grateful for the moments of freedom, which bring clarity and motivation to my days.

I think it’s important to note that I am not a raven; rather, I am a displaced student who is invested in knowing how every computer system works from the inside out. The key to what I am trapped in is creation, specifically software and hardware creation. From the first time I created a key, the first currency converter popped up on my terminal (since that is the project I learned programming in general, specifically Python, through the Boy Scout Programming Merit Badge), to creating my more complex keys that break the new locks, my website, and AI engines to use AI APIs for terminal use I have seen and done a lot. I have an impulse to push forward and work at the forefront of technology, especially in software development and cybersecurity.
