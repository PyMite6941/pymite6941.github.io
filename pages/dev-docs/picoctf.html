What it is

picoCTF is a free capture-the-flag platform run by Carnegie Mellon University, originally built as a security competition for middle and high school students. Challenges are grouped by category — web exploitation, cryptography, reverse engineering, forensics, binary exploitation, and general skills — and each is worth points scaled to difficulty. Alongside the annual timed competition, picoCTF keeps a permanently open practice gym of retired challenges, which is what the solve log below draws from.
